MAIN REQUIREMENT — THREE ORDER STATES:

STATE B — DELIVERED BUT NOT RECEIVED
The system says the order was delivered, but the customer reports they did not receive it.
Requirements:
- Clearly show that the carrier/system marks the order as delivered.
- Clearly distinguish this from the customer's reported problem.
- Provide an appropriate next step.
- Include an obvious "Report an issue" / "I didn't receive this order" interaction.

- Provide a support escalation/action.

The order exists, but tracking information is not available yet.
